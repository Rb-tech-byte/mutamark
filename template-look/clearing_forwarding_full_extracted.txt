61. MODULE 43 — CLEARING & FORWARDING MANAGEMENT
Document status: Proposed addition to the Mutamark ERP master SRS. This module extends the existing ERP and reuses its CRM, supplier, procurement, inventory, accounting, document management, approval, communication, customer portal, reporting and audit capabilities.
Purpose: Manage the complete import, export, transit and domestic cargo forwarding lifecycle, from customer instruction and document collection through compliance checks, customs processing, cargo release, transport, tracking and signed cargo handover.
Operating principle: One shipment/job file is the single source of truth for every instruction, document, declaration reference, payment, milestone, exception, cost, communication and handover record associated with a cargo movement.
61.1 Objectives and business outcomes
• Create a centralized electronic job file for every import, export, transit or forwarding assignment.
• Control document collection, verification, permits, classification and valuation review, declaration records and supporting evidence.
• Track customs processing and regulatory approvals without replacing official government systems or authority decisions.
• Track cargo from shipper/origin through port, airport, border, warehouse and final consignee destination.
• Manage transport orders, trucks, drivers, loading, dispatch, border events, delivery and signed proof of delivery.
• Record statutory and third-party charges, customer advances, disbursements, invoices, receipts, refunds and job profitability.
• Reduce missing documents, duplicate entry, avoidable delays, demurrage, detention and unapproved expenditure.
• Provide customers with shipment status, document access, quotations, invoices and proof of delivery.
• Maintain a complete, searchable audit trail.
61.2 Scope and shipment types
The module shall support configurable service categories:
• Import clearance by sea, air, road and other approved modes.
• Export clearance and export forwarding.
• Transit cargo moving through Tanzania to or from another country.
• Regional cross-border road freight and forwarding.
• Domestic forwarding and inland transportation.
• Port, airport, ICD, CFS, border, bonded-warehouse and terminal coordination.
• Project cargo, oversized cargo, containerized cargo, bulk cargo, break-bulk and general cargo.
• Clearing-only, forwarding-only and end-to-end door-to-door assignments.
Shipment type, country, customs regime, transport mode, route, cargo category and required approvals shall be configurable. The exact operational process depends on applicable law, customs regime, cargo type and competent authority.

[TABLE]
Stage | Process | System output / control
1. Enquiry & instruction | Receive customer enquiry, service request and authority to act. | Enquiry record; customer instruction; job acceptance.

8. Declaration preparation | Prepare declaration data and supporting documents for authorized submission. | Declaration draft, reference and checklist.
9. Assessment & queries | Record assessment, customs queries, amendments, verification and inspection. | Assessment record; query log; action owner.

61.4 Detailed functional requirements
A. Customer enquiry, instruction and job registration
• FR-CF-001 The system shall create a clearing/forwarding enquiry linked to an existing or new customer.
• FR-CF-002 Capture customer instruction, service scope, cargo owner, shipper, consignee, notify party, importer/exporter and authorized contacts.
• FR-CF-003 Capture direction (import/export/transit/domestic), customs regime, service type, transport mode, Incoterms where applicable, origin, destination, route and requested delivery date.
• FR-CF-004 Generate a unique configurable job number, e.g. CF-IMP-2026-00001, CF-EXP-2026-00001 or CF-TRN-2026-00001.
• FR-CF-005 Assign job owner, operations officer, documentation officer, authorized declarant/agent, finance officer, transporter and approving manager.
• FR-CF-006 Detect possible duplicate jobs using customer, transport document, container or shipment references; allow authorized override with reason.
B. Shipment and cargo details
• FR-CF-007 Capture master/house bill of lading, airway bill, consignment note, booking, manifest and other applicable identifiers.
• FR-CF-008 Capture vessel/voyage, flight, shipping line/airline, ports, place of receipt/delivery, ETA, ATA, ETD and ATD where applicable.
• FR-CF-009 Cargo lines shall capture description, packages, marks/numbers, gross/net weight, volume, quantity, unit, proposed HS code, origin, export country, declared value and currency.
• FR-CF-010 Support multiple cargo lines, containers, packages, vehicles and transport legs under one job.
• FR-CF-011 Container record shall capture number, size/type, seal, status, pickup/return location, free-time dates and empty-return evidence.
• FR-CF-012 Vehicle cargo may capture chassis/VIN, registration, make/model and other identifiers.
• FR-CF-013 Record cargo condition, photographs, seal condition, shortages, excesses, damage and discrepancies at custody transfers.
C. Document collection and verification
• FR-CF-014 Provide configurable checklists by shipment type, regime, commodity, country, mode and customer.
• FR-CF-015 Support commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, airway bill, road consignment note, certificate of origin, insurance, permits, product certificates, inspection certificates, exemption evidence, tax details, agency appointment and other supporting documents.
• FR-CF-016 each document shall record type, reference, issuer, issue/expiry dates, received date, source party, format, verification status, version and related job/cargo.
• FR-CF-017 Accept PDF, Word, Excel, image and scanned documents; record whether original hardcopy is received, pending or not required.
• FR-CF-018 Identify missing, expired, unreadable, inconsistent or duplicate documents and generate an action list.
• FR-CF-019 Preserve original files, version history, uploader, timestamp and review comments; do not silently overwrite submitted versions.
• FR-CF-020 Optional OCR/data extraction may assist capture, but an authorized user must verify extracted data before customs or financial use.
• FR-CF-021 Generate customer document request lists and allow secure customer-portal uploads.
D. Compliance, permits, HS classification and valuation
• FR-CF-022 Maintain configurable regulatory requirements by cargo type, HS code, origin/destination, customs regime and effective date.
• FR-CF-023 Record required permits, licences, certificates, inspections and approvals with authority, application reference, dates, validity, status and evidence.
• FR-CF-024 Capture proposed HS code, tariff description, classification rationale, source/reference, reviewer and approval status.
• FR-CF-025 Treat HS classification as a controlled decision; retain history and never treat an automated suggestion as approved classification.
• FR-CF-026 Capture valuation inputs including transaction value, currency, freight, insurance and applicable additions/deductions.
• FR-CF-027 Record origin, origin evidence and preferential treatment claim/certificate where relevant.
• FR-CF-028 Provide configurable checks for restricted/prohibited goods, permit requirements, valuation anomalies and inconsistent descriptions.
• FR-CF-029 Provide warnings/escalation without overriding decisions reserved for customs or regulatory authorities.
E. Quotation, estimates and customer funding
• FR-CF-030 Prepare job estimates covering professional fees, duties, taxes, levies, port/terminal, shipping line, inspection, storage, handling, transport, insurance, bank and other charges.
• FR-CF-031 Classify charges as estimated, funded, incurred, invoiced, paid, refundable or disputed, with source evidence.
• FR-CF-032 Support multiple currencies, configurable exchange rates, effective dates and rate source/history.
• FR-CF-033 Support quotation approval, revision, expiry, customer acceptance and digital acknowledgement.
• FR-CF-034 Generate customer advance/funding requests before disbursement where company policy requires.
• FR-CF-035 Record customer funds received, allocate them to a job and maintain unspent-advance balances.
• FR-CF-036 Costs above approved estimates/tolerance require reason, evidence and approval, subject to a logged emergency override.
F. Customs declaration and processing register
• FR-CF-037 Provide a declaration workspace for regime/type, customs office, declarant, importer/exporter, cargo lines, HS codes, quantities, values, origin and transport references.
• FR-CF-038 Store declaration number, customs-system reference, submission date/time, submitting user, customs office and status.
• FR-CF-039 Support preparation, internal review, approval to submit, submission record, assessment, query, amendment, examination, release and cancellation/rejection history.
• FR-CF-040 Where an official customs platform has a supported and authorized API, exchange data securely; otherwise capture references and upload official evidence manually.
• FR-CF-041 Do not claim to submit declarations, make official payments or obtain release unless a verified authorized integration has completed that action.
• FR-CF-042 Retain every declaration version and amendment reason, including preparer, checker, authorizer and submitter.
• FR-CF-043 Record assessed amounts by charge type, payment references, customs queries, examination instructions and release references.
G. Inspections, port, terminal and border operations
• FR-CF-044 Schedule and record physical examination, scanning, sampling, weighbridge, verification and other inspection events.
• FR-CF-045 Capture authority, location, date/time, assigned officer, instructions, findings, photographs, seal changes, discrepancies and outcome.
• FR-CF-046 Maintain event logs for ports, airports, ICDs, CFSs, customs stations, border posts, bonded facilities and warehouses.
• FR-CF-047 Capture gate pass, exit note, release order, delivery order, terminal release, interchange receipt and other custody/gate documents.
• FR-CF-048 Border records shall capture border post, entry/exit country, arrival/departure time, vehicle/rotation/manifest references where applicable and inspection status.
• FR-CF-049 Record holds, queries, system outages, missing documents, inspection issues, congestion, demurrage and detention as assigned exceptions.
H. Transport and forwarding management
• FR-CF-050 Create transport orders linked to jobs, defining pickup, delivery, route, cargo, service level and instructions.
• FR-CF-051 Transporter profile shall include business details, contacts, tax details, licences/insurance evidence where applicable, bank/payment details, service areas and performance.
• FR-CF-052 Vehicle record shall include registration, type, capacity, transporter/owner, inspection/insurance/permit expiry and availability.
• FR-CF-053 Driver record shall include name, contact, licence details/expiry and operational identifiers required for the movement.
• FR-CF-054 Dispatch requires approved transport order, release confirmation where applicable, vehicle/driver assignment, loading confirmation and required transport documents.
• FR-CF-055 Capture route, checkpoints, border crossings, estimated/actual arrival, trip advances, tolls and trip expenses where used.
• FR-CF-056 Support GPS/telematics integration where provider and authorized API exist; retain manual milestone capture.
• FR-CF-057 Support multiple vehicles, partial deliveries, split consignments, transshipment, route changes and return trips.
• FR-CF-058 Record loading evidence, seal number, driver acknowledgement, cargo photos and dispatch time.
I. Tracking, exceptions and customer visibility
• FR-CF-059 Display a chronological shipment timeline from job opening to final handover.
• FR-CF-060 Each milestone shall record status, location, event time, responsible user/source, notes and attachment.
• FR-CF-061 Alert on document gaps, permit expiry, ETA changes, customs queries, assessment, payment due, release, delayed dispatch, border hold, late delivery and container return deadline.
• FR-CF-062 Calculate elapsed time by stage and flag jobs exceeding configured service targets.
• FR-CF-063 Customers may view permitted milestones and download released documents without access to internal notes or restricted data.
• FR-CF-064 Record customer notifications and communication history against the job.
J. Cargo delivery, proof of delivery and handover
• FR-CF-065 Generate delivery note/handover document linked to job, transport order, cargo lines and consignee.
• FR-CF-066 Capture delivery point, date/time, recipient name/organization/contact, lawful authority confirmation where appropriate, quantities/packages/containers and cargo condition.
• FR-CF-067 Recipient may acknowledge by device signature, approved electronic acknowledgement or signed/scanned hardcopy.
• FR-CF-068 Support partial delivery, rejected delivery, shortage, damage, seal discrepancy, refusal and redelivery workflows.
• FR-CF-069 POD shall include acknowledgement, photos where appropriate, GPS evidence where enabled, delivery note and remarks.
• FR-CF-070 Do not mark handover complete until required evidence and discrepancy disposition are recorded or an authorized exception is approved.
• FR-CF-071 Notify customer and operations team when delivery/handover is completed.
K. Job costing, billing and accounting integration
• FR-CF-072 Link job costs and income to existing accounting, AR, AP, cash/bank and tax modules.
• FR-CF-073 Distinguish agency/service revenue from statutory duties, taxes and third-party disbursements according to approved accounting policy.
• FR-CF-074 Record third-party invoices and match to estimates, purchase orders or approved cost commitments where applicable.
• FR-CF-075 Generate customer invoices, debit/credit notes, receipts and statements through existing ERP finance functions.
• FR-CF-076 Reconcile customer advances against actual job expenses and invoices, showing refundable or additional amounts.
• FR-CF-077 Calculate job profitability by customer, shipment, route, service, branch and period using approved accounting rules.
• FR-CF-078 Support foreign-currency transactions and exchange differences with rate audit history.
• FR-CF-079 Posted accounting entries shall use reversal/adjustment for corrections, not deletion.
L. Job closure and archive
• FR-CF-080 Closure checklist shall verify handover, final documents, official payment evidence, third-party invoices, customer billing, advance reconciliation, container/return obligations and claims.
• FR-CF-081 Distinguish operationally completed, financially pending and fully closed statuses.
• FR-CF-082 Closed jobs shall be read-only for ordinary users; reopening requires approval and reason.
• FR-CF-083 Archive complete job file with searchable metadata, document versions, approvals, communications, costs, declaration references and handover evidence.
61.5 Job statuses and workflow controls
[TABLE]
Status | Meaning / exit condition
Enquiry | Customer request received; scope not accepted.
Awaiting Instruction | Formal instruction/authority or required information pending.
Job Open | Job accepted, numbered and assigned.
Documents Pending | Required documents outstanding.
Under Document Review | Documents being checked for completeness and consistency.
Compliance Pending | Permits/certificates/regulatory approvals outstanding.
Ready for Declaration | Required information and internal checks complete.
Declaration Submitted | Submission reference and evidence recorded.
Under Customs Processing | Assessment, query, examination or processing ongoing.
Awaiting Payment | Assessment or approved charges await funding/payment.
Awaiting Release | Requirements/payment evidence recorded; release confirmation pending.
Released | Required cargo release evidence recorded.
Awaiting Transport | Transport planning/vehicle allocation pending.
In Transit | Cargo dispatched and movement underway.
At Delivery Point | Vehicle/cargo arrived for delivery.
Partially Delivered | Some cargo delivered; balance remains.
Delivered / Handover Complete | Required POD recorded.
Financial Reconciliation | Costs, advances and invoices being finalized.
Operationally Complete | Physical delivery complete; financial/admin closure remains.
Closed | Closure checklist approved and file archived.
On Hold / Cancelled | Paused or cancelled with reason, authority and audit record.
[/TABLE]

Status transitions shall be configurable but controlled. Mandatory checks cannot be skipped to reach Released, Delivered or Closed without required evidence or an approved exception.

• Quotation awaiting approval; customer advance below required funding; cost exceeding estimate.
• Declaration submitted, assessed, queried, selected for examination, amended or released.
• Official payment due or payment evidence unreconciled; ETA/ATA change; free-time/container-return deadline.

61.13 Business rules and mandatory controls
1. Every operational shipment must have a unique job number before processing activity is recorded.
2. A job must identify the customer, service scope, cargo owner/consignee and responsible officer.
3. Mandatory document requirements must be checked before Ready for Declaration.
4. HS code and valuation proposals must retain supporting references and review/approval where required; automated suggestions are not final decisions.
5. Only authorized users may prepare or record declaration submission and amendments.
6. Official assessment, duty/tax payment and release must have an official reference or evidence attachment.
7. Distinguish estimates from official assessed amounts and actual payments.
8. Cargo dispatch normally requires release and transport checks, unless an authorized documented exception applies.
9. Vehicle and driver assignment must satisfy configured operational validity checks.
10. Handover must record recipient acknowledgement and cargo condition; discrepancies must be documented.
11. Customer advances and disbursements must reconcile to the job and accounting records.
12. Closed jobs are read-only for ordinary users; reopening requires authorization and reason.
13. Audit records cannot be erased and approved document versions cannot be silently replaced.
61.14 Acceptance criteria and user acceptance testing
1. Authorized user can create enquiry, accept instruction and open a uniquely numbered job.
2. A job can contain multiple cargo lines, containers, transport documents and parties.
3. Document checklist identifies missing, expired, duplicate and unverified documents.
4. Permits and regulatory requirements can be recorded, tracked and evidenced.
5. Classification and valuation worksheets retain proposal, source, reviewer and approval history.
6. Quotation can be prepared, approved, sent and accepted by the customer.
7. Customer advances can be received, allocated, spent and reconciled against disbursements.
8. Declaration data, references, assessments, queries, amendments, inspections and release evidence can be recorded and audited.
9. Port, airport, warehouse and border events appear in a chronological timeline.
10. Transport orders can be created with transporter, vehicle, driver, route and dispatch checklist.
11. Authorized users can record milestones, exceptions and customer notifications.
12. Partial and full delivery can be recorded with quantities, condition, recipient acknowledgement and POD.
13. Job account reconciles estimates, actual costs, advances, invoices, receipts and outstanding balances.
14. Reports cover shipment register, document compliance, customs processing, transport, delivery, disbursement and profitability.
15. Role restrictions, segregation of duties, audit logs, backups and document controls pass security testing.
16. A complete job can be traced from customer instruction through document verification, customs processing, release, transport, signed cargo handover and financial closure.

61.17 Implementation assumptions and limitations
• This module describes ERP workflow and records. It does not replace customs-agent licensing, statutory procedures, official declarations or decisions by competent authorities.
• Tanzania-specific customs, tax, permit, port and border rules must be validated against current official requirements before production configuration.
• External platform names and reference data must be configurable and maintained as platforms and procedures change.
• External connectivity requires documented APIs, credentials, permissions, security review and approval from the platform owner.
• Tariff/tax rates, exemptions, valuation rules, restricted-goods lists, document requirements and retention periods must be configurable and effective-dated, not hard-coded as permanent assumptions.
• Business owners must approve charge catalogue, accounting treatment of statutory disbursements, customer advance policy, service targets and approval thresholds before go-live.
