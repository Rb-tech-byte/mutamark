Introduction
1.1 Purpose
This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) defines the complete functional, technical, operational, and security
requirements for the Mutamark ERP System. The system shall serve as the centralized digital operating platform for
Mutamark Enterprises, integrating sales, procurement, inventory, accounting, human resources, projects, assets,
document management, digital signatures, and business intelligence into a single unified environment.
“Enter information once, use it throughout the business.”

1.3 Intended Audience
This document is intended for system architects, database designers, software developers, quality assurance engineers,
project managers, and Mutamark Enterprises stakeholders.
1.4 Project Scope
The Mutamark ERP shall replace fragmented business processes with integrated, paperless, audit-ready workflows. The
system shall support multi-branch operations, role-based access, digital approvals, automated accounting entries, and
real-time management dashboards.

3
System Features
3.1 MODULE 1 — SYSTEM ADMINISTRATION
3.1.1 The system shall allow authorized administrators to configure: Company profile, branding, and letterhead; Financial years and
periods; Base currency and additional foreign currencies with exchange rates; Tax rates, tax groups, and tax-inclusive/exclusive pricing
rules; Payment methods and terms; Document numbering sequences and formats; Branches, warehouses, and departments; Units of
measure and product categories; System preferences and localization settings; Backup schedules and retention policies.
3.1.2 The system shall maintain an immutable audit log of all administrative configuration changes.
3.2 MODULE 2 — COMPANY & BRANCH MANAGEMENT
3.2.1 The system shall support: Management of one or more legal business entities; Multiple branches, stores, and warehouses per
business entity; Separate stock balances, sales, and purchase tracking by location; Branch-specific user assignments and permissions;
Branch-specific financial reporting; Consolidated group-level financial and operational reporting.
3.3 MODULE 3 — USER, ROLE & PERMISSION MANAGEMENT
3.3.1 The system shall implement Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) with configurable permissions for: View, Create, Edit, Delete,
Approve, Reject, Print, Export, Download, Send, Sign, Pay, Cancel, Reverse, Import.
3.3.2 Permissions shall be granularly configurable by: Module, branch, warehouse, department, transaction type, and transaction
amount threshold.
3.3.3 The system shall support: Unlimited user accounts; Custom role creation; Two-factor authentication (2FA); Strong password
enforcement; Session timeout and concurrent session management; Login history and failed-login protection.
3.4 MODULE 4 — CRM & CUSTOMER MANAGEMENT
3.4.1 The system shall maintain a Customer Master with: Customer ID, name, company name, contact person; Phone, email, physical
address, tax identification; Customer category, type (individual/company), assigned salesperson; Credit limit, payment terms, default
discount, opening balance; Attached documents and notes.
3.4.2 The system shall provide: Customer registration and history tracking; Customer grouping and categorization; Credit limit
enforcement with override controls; Customer statement generation (transactional and outstanding balance); Customer
communication history logging; Customer enquiry and follow-up tracking; Complaint and support ticket management.
3.5 MODULE 5 — SUPPLIER & VENDOR MANAGEMENT
3.5.1 The system shall maintain a Supplier Master with: Supplier ID, name, contact person, phone, email, address; Tax/VAT
information, bank details, payment terms; Supplier category, opening balance, performance rating; Attached documents and
qualification records.

--- PAGE 7 ---
3.5.2 The system shall provide: Supplier ledger with full transaction history; Purchase order, GRN, and invoice linkage; Supplier
payment tracking and advance management; Supplier aging and outstanding balance reports; Supplier performance evaluation and
rating.
3.6 MODULE 6 — PRODUCT & SERVICE CATALOG
3.6.1 The system shall support the following product types: General products; Variable products (with attributes); Services; Serial-
number products; IMEI-tracked products; Batch/lot-tracked products; Expiry-date products; Installment products; Combo/kit products.
3.6.2 The Product Master shall contain: Product ID, SKU (auto-generated), barcode, product name, alternative name; Description,
brand, category, sub-category, unit of measure; Purchase price, selling price, wholesale price, minimum price; Configurable profit
margin with automatic selling-price calculation; Tax assignment, reorder level, maximum stock, preferred supplier; Default warehouse,
product image; Serial number, batch number, and expiry date tracking where applicable; Warranty period.
3.7 MODULE 7 — INVENTORY & WAREHOUSE MANAGEMENT
3.7.1 The system shall maintain real-time stock balances across all warehouses with full traceability.
3.7.2 The system shall support the following stock transaction types: Opening stock; Purchase receipt; Sales issue; Sales return;
Purchase return; Warehouse-to-warehouse transfer; Stock adjustment (positive/negative); Stock count/physical verification; Damaged
stock write-off; Expired stock write-off; Stock issue for projects; Stock receipt from returns.
3.7.3 The system shall provide: Live stock status and valuation (FIFO, Average Cost, or Standard Cost); Low-stock and reorder-level
alerts; Stock movement history and ledger; Damaged, expired, and lost stock tracking; Barcode scanning for stock operations; Negative
stock prevention (configurable by product/warehouse).
3.8 MODULE 8 — PROCUREMENT MANAGEMENT (PROCURE-TO-PAY)
3.8.1 The system shall enforce the following workflow:
Purchase Requisition → Approval → RFQ → Supplier Quotations
→ Comparative Evaluation → Purchase Order → GRN
→ Supplier Invoice → Three-Way Matching → Payment → Supplier Statement
3.8.2 Purchase Requisition: Requisition number, department, requester, date, required date; Items, quantities, estimated prices,
purpose, budget reference; Attachment support and multi-level approval routing.
3.8.3 Request for Quotation (RFQ): Supplier selection, RFQ issuance, quotation receipt; Price, delivery period, and payment term
recording; Automatic comparative statement generation.
3.8.4 Purchase Order (PO): PO number, supplier, items, quantities, prices, discounts, taxes; Delivery terms, payment terms, delivery
location; Authorized signature placeholders; Automatic inventory commitment upon approval.
3.8.5 Goods Received Note (GRN): GRN creation against PO with quantity variance tracking; Automatic inventory update upon GRN
posting; Quality inspection status and rejection handling.
3.8.6 Three-Way Matching: The system shall enforce matching of PO, GRN, and Supplier Invoice before payment authorization, with
configurable tolerance for quantity and price variances.

--- PAGE 8 ---
3.8.7 Supplier Invoice & Payment: Supplier invoice recording and linkage to PO/GRN; Payment voucher generation, advance
payment tracking; Supplier payment scheduling and due-date alerts.
3.8.8 The system shall provide a procurement dashboard with pending requisitions, open POs, overdue deliveries, and purchase
budget utilization.
3.9 MODULE 9 — SALES MANAGEMENT (QUOTE-TO-CASH)
3.9.1 The system shall enforce the following workflow:
Customer Enquiry → Quotation → Customer Approval → Sales Order
→ Stock Allocation → Delivery Note → Invoice → Receipt/Payment
→ Customer Statement
3.9.2 Quotation: Creation, editing, approval, rejection, expiry date management; Email, PDF download, print, and WhatsApp sharing;
Digital signature application; Conversion to Sales Order or Invoice.
3.9.3 Proforma Invoice: Proforma generation, sharing, and conversion to formal invoice.
3.9.4 Sales Order: Customer confirmation tracking, stock reservation; Partial and full fulfillment management.
3.9.5 Delivery Note: Delivery creation from sales order or invoice; Partial delivery support, delivery confirmation; Serial/batch number
capture at point of delivery.
3.9.6 Invoice: Automatic invoice generation from delivery or sales order; Tax calculation, discount application, credit limit validation;
Automatic accounting entry generation (Dr. AR, Cr. Revenue, Cr. Tax); PDF generation with QR code for verification; Email, SMS,
WhatsApp distribution.
3.9.7 Receipt & Payment: Payment allocation to specific invoices; Advance receipt handling, overpayment management; Automatic
accounting entry (Dr. Bank/Cash, Cr. AR).
3.9.8 Credit & Debit Notes: Credit note issuance for returns or adjustments; Debit note issuance for additional charges.
3.9.9 The system shall provide sales return processing with automatic inventory and accounting reversal.
3.10 MODULE 10 — POINT OF SALE (POS)
3.10.1 The POS module shall support: Cash sales, credit sales, and multiple payment methods per transaction; Barcode scanning,
product search, and quick product selection; Customer selection, discount application, and tax calculation; Held/parked sales and
retrieval; Sales returns and refund processing; Thermal receipt printing, customer-facing display; Cash drawer management and POS
register balancing; Daily closing and Z-report generation; Cash reconciliation and variance reporting.
3.11 MODULE 11 — ACCOUNTING & FINANCE
3.11.1 The system shall implement double-entry accounting integrated with all commercial transactions.
3.11.2 Chart of Accounts: Hierarchical chart with categories: Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Revenue, Cost of Sales, Expenses; Account
codes, names, types, and parent-child relationships; Opening balance entry and financial year closing.

--- PAGE 9 ---
3.11.3 Core Accounting Functions: General Ledger with full transaction drill-down; Journal entries with approval workflow; Cash
book and bank account management; Accounts Receivable and Accounts Payable sub-ledgers; Trial Balance, Profit & Loss Statement,
Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement; Bank reconciliation with statement import; Opening and closing balance management.
3.11.4 Automated Accounting Entries: The system shall automatically generate accounting entries for: Sales invoices (Dr. AR, Cr.
Revenue, Cr. Tax Payable); Customer payments (Dr. Bank/Cash, Cr. AR); Purchase receipts (Dr. Inventory, Cr. AP); Supplier payments (Dr.
AP, Cr. Bank/Cash); Stock adjustments (Dr./Cr. Inventory, Cr./Dr. Adjustment Account); Expense payments (Dr. Expense, Cr. Bank/Cash);
Payroll posting (Dr. Salary Expense, Cr. Bank/Cash, Cr. Deductions Payable).
3.12 MODULE 12 — TAX MANAGEMENT
3.12.1 The system shall support: Configurable tax rates and tax groups; Tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive pricing models; Product-
specific and transaction-specific tax assignment; Sales tax, purchase tax, withholding tax tracking; Tax liability reports and tax return
support; Future-ready architecture for Tanzanian e-invoicing (e-TIMS) integration.
3.13 MODULE 13 — EXPENSE & INCOME MANAGEMENT
3.13.1 The system shall support configurable expense categories including: Rent, utilities, transport, communication, salaries, office
expenses, repairs, marketing, bank charges, taxes, and other expenses.
3.13.2 Each expense shall require: Expense request, approval workflow, payment voucher, and accounting entry.
3.13.3 The system shall track non-sales income sources with categorization and reporting.
3.14 MODULE 14 — CASH & BANK MANAGEMENT
3.14.1 The system shall manage: Multiple cash accounts and bank accounts; Deposits, withdrawals, and inter-account transfers; Bank
reconciliation with unreconciled transaction tracking; Cash reconciliation and float management; Payment vouchers and receipt
vouchers; Bank statement import (CSV, Excel, MT940 where applicable).
3.15 MODULE 15 — HUMAN RESOURCE MANAGEMENT
3.15.1 The system shall maintain an Employee Master with: Employee ID, personal details, employment details; Department, position,
employment status, contract details; Salary structure, allowances, deductions; Attached documents (contracts, certificates, IDs).
3.15.2 The system shall provide: Employee directory and organizational chart; Contract management and renewal alerts; Employee
performance tracking; Commission and incentive calculation; Employee exit/termination management.
3.16 MODULE 16 — ATTENDANCE & LEAVE MANAGEMENT
3.16.1 The system shall support: Daily check-in/check-out with timestamp; Attendance correction requests and approval; Late arrival,
early departure, and absence flagging; Leave type configuration (annual, sick, maternity, etc.); Leave application, approval workflow,
and balance tracking; Holiday calendar management; Attendance reports and payroll integration.
3.17 MODULE 17 — PAYROLL MANAGEMENT
3.17.1 The system shall calculate: Basic salary, allowances, deductions, overtime; Commissions, loans, and salary advances; Statutory
deductions (PAYE, NSSF, NHIF, WCF as applicable); Net salary.
3.17.2 The system shall generate: Payroll register and summary; Individual payslips (printable and downloadable); Salary history and
variance reports; Automatic accounting posting for payroll.

--- PAGE 10 ---
3.18 MODULE 18 — PROJECT MANAGEMENT
3.18.1 The system shall support: Project registration with unique project number; Customer assignment, project manager assignment;
Start date, end date, budget allocation; Actual expenditure tracking (materials, labor, expenses); Revenue recognition and profitability
calculation; Milestone definition and tracking; Project document repository; Customer portal viewing and comment functionality.
3.19 MODULE 19 — TASK MANAGEMENT
3.19.1 The system shall provide: Task creation, assignment, and deadline setting; Priority levels and status tracking: Draft → Assigned
→ In Progress → Review → Completed → Closed; Comment threads and file attachments; Calendar view and milestone linkage;
Completion percentage and time logging.
3.20 MODULE 20 — FIXED ASSET MANAGEMENT
3.20.1 The system shall manage: Asset registration with unique asset code; Asset category, purchase cost, purchase date, location;
Responsible employee/custodian, supplier, warranty details; Depreciation calculation (straight-line, reducing balance, or custom
methods); Asset transfer between locations or employees; Asset disposal and write-off; Physical verification tracking and asset history
log.
3.21 MODULE 21 — WARRANTY & SERVICING
3.21.1 The system shall support: Warranty registration linked to product serial numbers and invoices; Warranty period tracking and
expiry alerts; Customer warranty lookup by invoice, customer name, or phone number; Service request intake and repair ticket
management; Vendor repair workflow (send to vendor, track status, receive back); Paid servicing billing and customer notification;
Complete service history per product/serial number.
3.22 MODULE 22 — DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (DMS)
3.22.1 The system shall provide a central electronic document repository attaching files to: Customers, suppliers, employees,
products; Purchases, sales, projects, assets, payments, contracts.
3.22.2 Supported formats shall include PDF, Word, Excel, images, and scanned documents.
3.22.3 The system shall maintain document version history with upload timestamp and user attribution.
3.23 MODULE 23 — DIGITAL SIGNATURE & VERIFICATION
3.23.1 The system shall integrate digital signatures into document workflows.
3.23.2 Each digital signature shall record: Signatory name and position; Date and timestamp; Document ID and version; Signature
status and verification identifier; IP address and device information where appropriate.
3.23.3 Signed documents shall display: Digital approval block with signatory details; Verification ID and QR code for third-party
verification.
3.23.4 The system shall provide a public verification page where any party can verify document authenticity using the document
number and verification code.
3.24 MODULE 24 — APPROVAL WORKFLOW ENGINE
3.24.1 The system shall provide a configurable workflow engine with statuses: Draft → Submitted → Under Review → Returned →
Approved → Rejected → Signed → Issued → Closed.

--- PAGE 11 ---
3.24.2 Approval rules shall support: User-based, role-based, and department-based routing; Amount-based escalation (e.g., ≤500,000
Procurement; 500,001–5,000,000 Manager; >5,000,000 Director); Sequential and parallel approval paths; Delegated approval during
absence; Segregation of duties (a user shall not approve their own transactions where prohibited).
3.24.3 All approval actions shall be recorded in the audit trail with before/after values.
3.25 MODULE 25 — PAPERLESS OFFICE & TEMPLATE ENGINE
3.25.1 The system shall minimize physical document dependency through digital workflow: Prepare → Review → Approve → Digitally
Sign → Generate PDF → Send → Archive.
3.25.2 The template engine shall support dynamic field substitution for: Company details, customer/supplier details, document
numbers, dates; Line items, subtotals, taxes, grand totals, amounts in words; Prepared by, approved by, signature blocks.
3.25.3 Templates shall be available for: Quotation, Proforma Invoice, Sales Order, Delivery Note, Invoice, Receipt; Purchase
Requisition, RFQ, Comparative Statement, Purchase Order, GRN; Credit Note, Debit Note, Payment Voucher, Receipt Voucher, Journal
Voucher; Customer Statement, Supplier Statement, Payslip, Contracts.
3.26 MODULE 26 — NOTIFICATION & COMMUNICATION ENGINE
3.26.1 The system shall generate automatic alerts for: Low stock, reorder levels, stock expiry; Overdue customer invoices and supplier
payments; Quotation expiry, contract expiry, warranty expiry; Pending approvals, pending deliveries, pending purchase orders;
Employee leave requests, payroll processing reminders; Task and project deadline reminders.
3.26.2 Notification channels shall include: In-system notification center; Email, SMS, WhatsApp messaging; Mobile push notifications
(via PWA).
3.27 MODULE 27 — CUSTOMER SELF-SERVICE PORTAL
3.27.1 Customers shall be able to: Securely log in with username/password or token-based access; View quotations, approve
quotations, and request revisions; View sales orders, delivery notes, and invoices; Download documents and view account statements;
Make online payments and track payment history; Submit support tickets and track order status.
3.28 MODULE 28 — SUPPLIER SELF-SERVICE PORTAL
3.28.1 Suppliers shall be able to: Receive RFQs and submit quotations; Receive purchase orders and confirm acceptance; Upload
invoices and delivery notes; View payment status and purchase history; Update company information and contact details.
3.29 MODULE 29 — BARCODE, QR & SERIAL MANAGEMENT
3.29.1 The system shall support: Barcode generation and label printing (standard formats: Code 128, EAN, UPC); QR code generation
for document verification and product tracking; Barcode scanning for stock receipt, stock issue, and POS operations; Serial number
and IMEI scanning and validation; Stock-taking via barcode scanner.
3.30 MODULE 30 — REPORTING & BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE
3.30.1 Sales Reports: Daily, monthly, and period sales reports; Sales by customer, product, branch, salesperson; Sales return analysis
and salesperson performance.
3.30.2 Purchase Reports: Purchase summary, supplier purchase history; Product purchase analysis, purchase returns; Supplier
performance and price variance.
3.30.3 Inventory Reports: Stock balance, stock movement, stock valuation; Low stock, damaged stock, expired stock; Warehouse-
wise stock and transfer reports.

3.30.4 Financial Reports: Trial Balance, General Ledger, Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow; Accounts Receivable aging, Accounts
Payable aging; Expense analysis and budget vs. actual.
3.30.5 HR Reports: Employee directory, attendance summary, leave balance; Payroll register, salary history, commission reports.
3.30.6 All reports shall support PDF export, Excel/CSV download, and date range filtering.
3.31 MODULE 31 — MANAGEMENT DASHBOARD
3.31.1 The dashboard shall display real-time KPIs including:

3.32 MODULE 32 — API & EXTERNAL INTEGRATION
3.32.1 The system shall provide a secure REST API with: OAuth 2.0 or API key authentication; Endpoints for customers, suppliers,
products, inventory, sales, purchases, invoices, payments, accounting, employees; Webhook support for event-driven integrations; Rate
limiting and request logging.
3.32.2 The system shall support data import/export via Excel and CSV templates.
3.33 MODULE 33 — PAYMENT INTEGRATION
3.33.1 The system shall support configurable payment methods: Cash, Bank transfer, Card, Mobile money (M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel
Money); Online payment gateways; Payment links for customer portal invoices.
3.33.2 Each payment shall be automatically linked to the originating invoice, customer/supplier account, and cash/bank ledger.
3.34 MODULE 34 — BACKUP & DISASTER RECOVERY
3.34.1 The system shall provide: Automated daily incremental backups and periodic full backups; Manual backup initiation; Backup
encryption and off-site storage; Point-in-time restore capability; Backup history and integrity verification; Documented disaster
recovery procedures.
3.35 MODULE 35 — AUDIT TRAIL & COMPLIANCE
3.35.1 The system shall record every sensitive transaction with: User identity, action type, date/time, IP address; Document number,
previous value, new value; Approval status change.
3.35.2 Audit records shall be immutable and non-deletable through standard application functions.
3.35.3 The system shall provide audit trail filtering and export for compliance and forensic review.

9
Acceptance Criteria
The ERP shall be considered ready for production when the following criteria are satisfied across all three project
tracks:
1. Authorized users can securely authenticate and access permitted functions.
2. Role-based permissions correctly restrict access by module, branch, and transaction amount.
3. Products, customers, and suppliers can be fully managed with complete history.
4. Purchase transactions update inventory and accounts payable automatically.
5. Sales transactions update inventory, accounts receivable, and revenue automatically.
6. Customer and supplier payments correctly update balances and bank/cash ledgers.
7. Financial reports reconcile to the transaction level with zero unexplained variances.
8. Approval workflows route correctly through configured hierarchies.
9. Digital signatures can be applied, displayed, and independently verified.
10. Documents can be generated as PDF, distributed electronically, and archived.
11. Audit logs capture all sensitive actions with before/after values.
12. Reports can be generated, filtered, and exported in PDF and Excel formats.
13. Data can be backed up, verified, and restored within documented RTO/RPO.
14. Unauthorized access attempts are prevented and logged.
15. Multiple branches operate independently while consolidated reporting reflects group position.
16. System performance remains acceptable under expected concurrent user load.
17. Critical workflows have been executed, server deployed, and signed off by Mutamark management.
